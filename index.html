The counter-intuitive bit

A mic that knows when to stop.

Most microphones are designed to capture everything. The Shy Mic does the opposite: it watches the sound level, and when a user-defined threshold is crossed, it mutes itself for three seconds before bravely returning.

It is part recorder, part experiment, and entirely unnecessary in the best possible way.
